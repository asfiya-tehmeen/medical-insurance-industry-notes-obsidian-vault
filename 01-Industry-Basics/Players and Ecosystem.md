# Players and Ecosystem

## Payers
- **Big national carriers**: UnitedHealthcare, Elevance (Anthem), Cigna, Aetna (CVS), Humana, Centene, Molina, Kaiser (integrated payer+provider), many regional **Blues** (BCBS, independent licensees).
- **Government**: Medicare (CMS), Medicaid (states), VA, TRICARE.
- **TPAs & administrators**: process claims for self-funded employers.

## Providers
Hospitals/health systems, physician groups, urgent care, labs (Quest, Labcorp), imaging, home health, pharmacies, behavioral health. Billed via **professional (CMS-1500 / 837P)** vs **institutional (UB-04 / 837I)** claims.

## Pharmacy chain
Manufacturers → wholesalers → pharmacies; PBMs (Express Scripts, CVS Caremark, Optum Rx) sit between plans and pharmacies.

## Intermediaries
- **Clearinghouses** (Change Healthcare, Availity, Waystar, Optum): validate/route EDI claims.
- **Brokers/agents**: sell plans.
- **Revenue Cycle Management (RCM)** vendors: help providers bill and collect.
- **Health IT vendors**: Epic, Oracle Health (Cerner) for EHRs; payer cores: Facets, QNXT, HealthEdge, Guidewire-like systems.
- **Analytics/risk vendors**: Optum, Cotiviti, Inovalon, Verisk.

## Regulators & standards bodies
CMS, HHS/OCR, ONC (health IT), state Departments of Insurance, DOL (ERISA), NAIC (state insurance commissioners), NCQA & URAC (accreditation), HL7 (FHIR), X12 (EDI), WEDI/CAQH CORE (operating rules), AMA (CPT), WHO/CDC (ICD).

## Where data/compliance people sit
Analytics/Actuarial, Data Engineering/Platform, Provider Data Management, Claims Operations IT, Risk Adjustment, Quality (HEDIS/Stars), Regulatory Reporting, Compliance & Privacy Office, Fraud/SIU analytics, Security/GRC, Interoperability/API teams.
