# Key Metrics and KPIs

## Financial
| Metric | Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **PMPM** | total cost ÷ member months | Standard cost unit; compare across populations |
| **MLR / MBR** | (medical claims + quality spend) ÷ premium | ACA floor 80/85% |
| **Admin ratio** | admin expense ÷ premium | |
| **Combined ratio** | MLR + admin ratio | <100% = underwriting profit |
| **IBNR** | estimated unreported claims | Reserves |
| **Premium PMPM, Net margin** | | |
| **Trend** | % change in PMPM YoY | Unit cost × utilization × mix |

## Utilization (per 1,000 members per year)
- **Admits/1000** = admissions ÷ member months × 12,000
- **Bed days/1000**, **ALOS** (avg length of stay)
- **ER visits/1000**, **Office visits/1000**, **Rx scripts/1000**
- **Readmission rate** (30-day all-cause), **Avoidable ER** rate
- **Imaging, specialty drug spend**

## Operations
- Claims turnaround time (days received→paid), **auto-adjudication rate**, **first-pass rate**, **denial rate**, **overturn rate**, **pend inventory**, **clean claim rate**, **rework rate**, payment accuracy (audited, goal 97%+)
- Call center: ASA, abandonment, FCR
- PA: turnaround, approval %, volume

## Network
Network adequacy (% members within X miles), provider directory accuracy, **in-network utilization rate** (leakage), average discount off billed/charges vs % of Medicare.

## Quality / Risk
HEDIS rates, Star ratings, avg **RAF**, **gap closure rate**, member NPS, CAHPS.

## Membership
Enrollment, **retention/churn**, new vs terminated, mix by age/gender/plan, **member months**.

## Cost drivers: "Price × Volume × Mix"
Decompose trend: e.g., +8% PMPM = +3% unit price + 4% utilization + 1% mix/intensity. Great interview answer.

## Concentration
Top 1% of members ≈ 20-25% of spend, top 5% ≈ 50%. High-cost claimants (>$100k) get special reporting.

## Worked example
Jan: 10,000 members, Feb: 10,200, Mar: 10,100 = 30,300 member months. Paid claims $12.1M. PMPM = $12.1M ÷ 30,300 = **$399.34**. Premium PMPM $480 → MLR = 399.34/480 = **83.2%**.

## Free sources to benchmark
KFF, CMS National Health Expenditure, MEPS (https://meps.ahrq.gov), HCUP (https://hcup-us.ahrq.gov), Milliman Medical Index.
