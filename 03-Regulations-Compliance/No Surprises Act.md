# No Surprises Act

**Problem:** You go to an in-network hospital, but the anesthesiologist is out-of-network and bills you the difference ("balance bill").

**Law:** For emergency services, and non-emergency services at in-network facilities by out-of-network providers, the patient pays only in-network cost-sharing. Provider and plan settle the rest.

## Pieces
- **Independent Dispute Resolution (IDR)**: arbiter picks between offers ("baseball-style"), anchored on the **Qualifying Payment Amount (QPA)**, roughly the plan's median in-network rate. QPA calculation is a data-heavy task (needs full contracted-rate history by code, geography, market).
- **Good Faith Estimates** for uninsured/self-pay patients; **Advanced EOB** for insured.
- **Provider directory** accuracy: update within 2 business days of provider info; verify every 90 days.
- **Continuity of care** when providers leave networks.
- **Air ambulance** coverage.
- ID cards must show deductible and OOP max.

## Why data people care
QPA computation, claims flagging (which claims are surprise-eligible), dispute volumes, directory validation, and reporting to CMS.
