# Compliance Programs, Audits, Reporting

## What a compliance department does
Policies and procedures, training, monitoring & auditing, reporting channel (hotline), investigations, regulatory change management, vendor oversight, filings with regulators.

**Three lines of defense**: (1) business operations own risk, (2) compliance/risk monitors, (3) internal audit independently tests.

## Common audits
| Audit | By | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| CMS Program Audit (MA/Part D) | CMS | Appeals, grievances, formulary, UM, enrollment, FDR oversight, CPE |
| RADV | CMS | Risk-adjustment diagnoses supported by records |
| Market Conduct Exam | State DOI | Claims handling, complaints, marketing, rating |
| HIPAA audit / investigation | OCR | Privacy/security safeguards |
| HEDIS compliance audit | NCQA auditors | Measure reporting accuracy |
| SOC 2 / HITRUST assessment | External | Security controls |
| Financial / MLR audit | State/CMS | Rebate calculations |
| Internal audit | Internal | Controls testing |

## Reporting you may build
MLR reports, Medicare Part C/D reporting requirements (many sections: grievances, appeals, PA, enrollment), Medicaid encounter data completeness, state quality reports, Transparency in Coverage files, ACA Section 1095 forms, NSA metrics, PA public metrics, network adequacy filings, Form 5500 for plans.

## Data-quality angle (this is where you shine)
- Reconcile counts across systems (claims received vs claims loaded vs claims reported).
- **Data lineage** and traceability to prove how a reported number was produced.
- **Reproducibility**: same inputs + code version = same output; keep snapshots and approvals.
- Controls: row-count checks, referential integrity, threshold alerts, sign-offs, segregation of duties, change management.
- Evidence packages for auditors: queries, screenshots, logs, sampling methods.

## Sampling basics
Auditors test a random or risk-based sample (e.g., 30-150 cases). You'll pull "universes" (full list of eligible cases), de-duplicate, and ensure timestamps and statuses are right. Universe errors are a top audit failure cause: "universe integrity".

## Corrective Action Plans (CAPs)
Findings are classified (observation, condition, corrective action required, immediate corrective action). Root-cause analysis, owners, deadlines, validation testing.

## Frameworks
COSO internal control, NIST CSF/800-53, ISO 27001, HITRUST, SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria.

## Careers
Compliance analyst, regulatory reporting analyst, privacy analyst, GRC analyst, audit analyst, program integrity analyst. Certs: CHC (Certified in Healthcare Compliance), CHPC (privacy), CIPP/US, CISA, HCISPP. See [[Certifications and Courses]].
