# ACA and Other Key Laws

## ACA (Affordable Care Act, 2010)
- **Guaranteed issue**: no denial for pre-existing conditions.
- **Community rating**: price varies only by age (max 3:1), tobacco (1.5:1), geography, family size.
- **Essential Health Benefits**, no lifetime/annual dollar limits, **preventive services at $0**.
- **Dependent coverage to age 26**.
- **Marketplaces + subsidies** (APTC tax credits, cost-sharing reductions).
- **Medical loss ratio** minimum (80/85%) with rebates.
- **Individual & employer mandates** (federal individual penalty is $0 since 2019; employer mandate remains for 50+ FTE employers; reporting via IRS forms 1094/1095).
- **Risk adjustment, reinsurance, risk corridors** (the "3 Rs").
- **Rate review** for premium increases.
- Section 1557 nondiscrimination.

## Medicare & Medicaid rules (CMS)
- Medicare Advantage / Part D: bids, marketing rules, network adequacy, Star Ratings, RADV, program audits, 42 CFR Parts 422/423.
- Medicaid managed care: 42 CFR Part 438 (access, quality, encounter data, rate setting).
- **CMS Interoperability & Patient Access Final Rule (2020)** + **Interoperability and Prior Authorization Rule (2024)**: payers must offer FHIR Patient Access API, Provider Access API, Payer-to-Payer exchange, Prior Auth API (phased 2026-2027), and publicly report PA metrics. Read: https://www.cms.gov/priorities/key-initiatives/burden-reduction/interoperability

## ERISA (1974)
Federal law for employer-sponsored plans: fiduciary duties, claims & appeals procedures, disclosure. Pre-empts many state laws for self-funded plans. Enforced by Dept. of Labor.

## [[No Surprises Act]] (2020, effective 2022)
Protects against surprise out-of-network bills; creates independent dispute resolution; requires good-faith estimates and directory accuracy.

## Mental Health Parity (MHPAEA)
Behavioral health benefits can't be more restrictive than medical/surgical. Plans must do **NQTL comparative analyses**, a hot audit area.

## Transparency in Coverage + Hospital Price Transparency
Machine-readable negotiated-rate files (payers), price-estimator tools, standard charge files (hospitals).

## COBRA, HIPAA portability, GINA, ADA
COBRA: temporary continuation after job loss. GINA: no genetic discrimination.

## State laws
Each state's Department of Insurance: licensing, form/rate filing, **prompt-pay** statutes, mandated benefits, market conduct exams, data breach laws, **consumer privacy** (e.g., CCPA/CPRA in California; Washington My Health My Data Act), **NYDFS cybersecurity regulation 23 NYCRR 500**.

## Other frameworks you'll hear
- **42 CFR Part 2**: extra privacy for substance-use-disorder records.
- **HITECH Act**: strengthened HIPAA, EHR incentives, breach notification.
- **21st Century Cures Act**: info-blocking prohibition, patient access APIs.
- **SOC 2**, **HITRUST CSF**, **NIST CSF**, **ISO 27001**: security frameworks; HITRUST is common among payers/vendors.
- **PCI DSS** if card payments; **SOX** for public companies.
- **GDPR**: only if EU data.
