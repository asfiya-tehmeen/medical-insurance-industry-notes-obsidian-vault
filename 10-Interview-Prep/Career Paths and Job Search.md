# Career Paths and Job Search

## Roles (data + compliance focus)
| Role | Does | Key skills |
|---|---|---|
| Healthcare Data Analyst | Dashboards, ad hoc claims analysis | SQL, BI, domain |
| Data Engineer (Payer) | Pipelines, warehouse, EDI | SQL, Python, Airflow, Snowflake/Databricks, X12 |
| Analytics Engineer | dbt models, metrics layer | SQL, dbt, modeling |
| Regulatory Reporting Analyst | CMS/state filings | SQL, Excel, regulations, controls |
| Compliance / Privacy Analyst | HIPAA program, audits | HIPAA, risk assessment, policy writing |
| Data Governance / Quality Analyst | Catalog, lineage, DQ rules | Collibra/Alation, SQL |
| Risk Adjustment Analyst | HCC/RAF, RADV | ICD-10, CMS rules, SQL |
| Quality Analyst (HEDIS) | Measures, gap lists | HEDIS specs, SQL |
| Program Integrity / FWA Analyst | Fraud analytics | SQL, anomaly detection |
| Interoperability Engineer | FHIR APIs, mappings | FHIR, HL7, APIs |
| Actuarial Analyst | Pricing, reserves | Stats, exams (SOA/CAS), SAS/R |
| Security/GRC Analyst | Controls, SOC2/HITRUST | frameworks |
| Business Analyst (Claims IT) | Requirements for claim systems | domain + SQL |

## Where to apply
Payers (UnitedHealth/Optum, Elevance, Cigna/Evernorth, CVS/Aetna, Humana, Centene, Molina, Kaiser, BCBS plans), PBMs, health-tech (Oscar, Clover, Bright, Devoted, Zocdoc, Cotiviti, Inovalon, Change Healthcare, Availity, Waystar, HealthEdge), consultancies (Deloitte, Accenture, Milliman, Oliver Wyman, Guidehouse), government contractors and state agencies. Boards: LinkedIn, Indeed, Health eCareers, company sites. Search keywords: "claims data analyst", "payer", "regulatory reporting", "HEDIS", "risk adjustment", "EDI", "FHIR", "privacy analyst".

## Resume tips
- Lead with domain + tools: "Built claims pipeline (837→Snowflake) with 99.9% reconciliation accuracy; HIPAA-aware design".
- Include projects from [[Portfolio Projects]] with metrics.
- Mirror vocabulary from job posts (PMPM, HEDIS, adjudication, encounter data).
- List certs and training (HIPAA, AHIP).

## Networking
LinkedIn messages to analysts/engineers at payers ("I'm studying claims data and built X; could I ask 2 questions?"), HIMSS chapters, HL7 working groups, Meetups. Share what you build.

## 30-60-90 day plan once hired
30: learn systems, data dictionary, key reports, who owns what. 60: fix one data issue end to end, document it. 90: propose and ship an automation or quality control with measurable impact.

## Salary and market info
Check levels.fyi, Glassdoor, BLS Occupational Outlook (https://www.bls.gov/ooh/). Healthcare data demand is strong due to interoperability mandates, price transparency, AI, and value-based care.
