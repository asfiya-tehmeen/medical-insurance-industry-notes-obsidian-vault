# Deals and M&A to Know

Confidence tags: **[Verified 2026]** found in 2026 reporting during research. **[Background]** from my general knowledge (older deals, fairly well known) but not re-checked. Confirm numbers before quoting. Always say "reportedly" for pending deals.

## Why deals matter to you
Mergers create **data integration work** (merging member/provider/claims systems), **compliance work** (new regulators, privacy, HIPAA BAAs, antitrust conditions), and **news you're expected to know** in interviews. Vertical integration (payer + PBM + clinics) is the dominant theme.

## How to talk about deals in an interview
Use this 4-part pattern: **What** (who bought whom) → **Why** (scale, vertical integration, data, margin) → **Risk** (antitrust, integration, regulatory, privacy) → **What it means for data/compliance** (system consolidation, MDM, BAAs, new reporting).
Example: "UnitedHealth's Change Healthcare acquisition gave it enormous claims-transaction data and connectivity. The 2024 attack showed the concentration risk, which is why third-party risk management and business continuity are now top compliance priorities."
