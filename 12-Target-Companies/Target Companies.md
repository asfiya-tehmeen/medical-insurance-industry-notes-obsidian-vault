## How to research any company (30 min)
1. **Latest earnings release + call transcript** (SEC EDGAR: https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/). Note: membership, medical-cost ratio, guidance, strategy keywords.
2. **Business lines & membership by segment** (10-K item 1). 
3. **Recent news & deals** (Fierce Healthcare, Becker's Payer, Healthcare Dive).
4. **Regulatory issues**: search "[company] DOJ / OCR / CMS sanctions / lawsuit".
5. **Tech stack**: job postings (Snowflake? Databricks? Azure? Facets?), engineering blogs, LinkedIn profiles.
6. **Your angle**: connect a company pain (e.g., Humana's Stars, Centene's Medicaid redeterminations) to a project or skill you have.

## Where to find jobs
Company career pages, LinkedIn, Indeed, Health eCareers (https://www.healthecareers.com), HIMSS JobMine (https://www.himss.org), Built In, Dice, state government job portals, USAJOBS (https://www.usajobs.gov). Search keywords: "claims data analyst", "payment integrity analyst", "regulatory reporting", "HEDIS analyst", "risk adjustment analyst", "EDI analyst", "data governance healthcare", "privacy analyst", "FHIR".

## Prioritization suggestion
Apply broadly to Tiers 1, 2, and 4 (vendors hire more junior/entry-level and teach you the domain), and use Tier 5 for compliance-heavy experience.
