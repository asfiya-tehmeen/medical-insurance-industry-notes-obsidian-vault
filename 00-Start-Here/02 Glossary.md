# Glossary (plain English)

**Payer**: the company that pays for care (insurer, Medicare, Medicaid). 
**Provider**: doctor, hospital, clinic, lab, pharmacy. 
**Member / Enrollee / Beneficiary**: the insured person. (Beneficiary is used for Medicare.) 
**Subscriber**: the main policyholder; **dependents** are spouse/kids on the plan. 
**Premium**: monthly price to keep coverage. 
**Deductible**: what you pay yourself each year before insurance starts sharing costs. 
**Copay**: fixed fee per visit (e.g., $30). 
**Coinsurance**: percentage you pay after deductible (e.g., 20%). 
**Out-of-pocket maximum (OOPM)**: yearly cap on what you pay; insurer pays 100% after. 
**Allowed amount**: the max the plan considers payable for a service (negotiated rate). 
**Billed charges**: what the provider asks; usually much higher than allowed. 
**Balance billing**: provider bills you the difference between billed and allowed (restricted by law for in-network and by the No Surprises Act). 
**In-network / Out-of-network**: providers with / without a contract with the plan. 
**Formulary**: list of drugs a plan covers, in tiers. 
**Prior authorization (PA)**: insurer must approve before a service. 
**EOB**: Explanation of Benefits: statement telling the member how a claim was processed. 
**Claim**: a bill submitted for payment. **Adjudication**: deciding to pay, deny, or adjust it. 
**Denial**: claim not paid. **Appeal**: request to reverse a denial. 
**COB (Coordination of Benefits)**: deciding which insurer pays first when someone has two. 
**Subrogation**: insurer recovers money from a third party at fault (e.g., car accident insurer). 
**Underwriting**: assessing risk to price/accept coverage. 
**Risk pool**: group of people whose costs are shared. 
**Adverse selection**: sick people buy insurance more than healthy ones, raising costs. 
**Actuary**: person who prices risk with statistics. 
**PMPM**: per member per month, the standard cost unit. 
**MLR / MBR**: medical loss ratio / medical benefit ratio = medical claims paid ÷ premiums. 
**ASO (Administrative Services Only)**: employer self-funds claims; insurer just administers. 
**Fully insured**: insurer takes the risk. **Self-funded**: employer takes the risk. 
**TPA**: Third-Party Administrator that processes claims for self-funded plans. 
**PBM**: Pharmacy Benefit Manager, middleman for drug benefits. 
**HMO / PPO / EPO / POS / HDHP**: plan types; see [[Plan Types and Cost Sharing]]. 
**ACA**: Affordable Care Act (2010). **Marketplace/Exchange**: where individuals buy ACA plans. 
**Medicare**: federal insurance for 65+/disabled (Parts A, B, C, D). 
**Medicaid**: state+federal program for low-income people. 
**CHIP**: children's health insurance program. 
**CMS**: Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, the federal regulator. 
**HHS / OCR**: Dept. of Health & Human Services / Office for Civil Rights (enforces HIPAA). 
**PHI**: Protected Health Information. **PII**: personally identifiable info. 
**HIPAA**: US privacy/security/transactions law. 
**EDI**: Electronic Data Interchange (the old-school file formats claims travel in). 
**X12 837/835/834/270/271/276/277**: standard EDI transactions; see [[X12 EDI Transactions]]. 
**ICD-10**: diagnosis codes. **CPT/HCPCS**: procedure codes. **NDC**: drug codes. **DRG**: hospital stay grouping. 
**NPI**: National Provider Identifier. **TIN**: tax ID. 
**HEDIS**: quality measures set. **Star Ratings**: CMS 1-5 star scores for Medicare plans. 
**HCC / RAF**: Hierarchical Condition Category / Risk Adjustment Factor, a risk score. 
**FHIR**: modern healthcare API standard. 
**Clearinghouse**: middleman that cleans and routes claims between providers and payers. 
**Utilization Management (UM)**: controlling unnecessary care (PA, reviews). 
**Case management / Care management**: nurses helping high-need members. 
**SIU**: Special Investigations Unit (fraud). **FWA**: Fraud, Waste, Abuse. 
**Capitation**: paying a provider a fixed amount per member instead of per service. 
**Fee-for-service (FFS)**: pay per service. **Value-based care (VBC)**: pay for outcomes. 
**IBNR**: Incurred But Not Reported, claims that happened but haven't arrived yet (a reserve). 
**Run-out**: period after year end when late claims still arrive. 
**Grace period**: time to pay premium before coverage ends. 
**SBC**: Summary of Benefits and Coverage. **EOC**: Evidence of Coverage. 
**SOC 2 / HITRUST**: security certifications common in vendors.
