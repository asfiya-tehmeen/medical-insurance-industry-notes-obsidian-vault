## One-paragraph version of the industry
People pay a monthly fee (**premium**) to an insurer (**payer**). When they get care from a doctor or hospital (**provider**), the provider sends a bill (**claim**). The payer checks the claim against the member's plan rules and the law, pays the allowed amount, and the member pays their share (**deductible/copay/coinsurance**). Everything in the industry is a variation on that loop, wrapped in heavy regulation and lots of data exchange.

## Why data + compliance is a great niche
- Insurers are data companies in disguise: claims, eligibility, pharmacy, labs, risk scores.
- Every step is regulated, so someone must prove data is correct, private, and reported on time.
- Skills: SQL, Python, ETL, data quality, plus knowing *why* a field exists. The domain knowledge is the moat.
