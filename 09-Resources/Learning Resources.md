# Learning Resources

## Start here (free, beginner-friendly)
- KFF Health Policy 101 / Health Insurance explainer: https://www.kff.org/health-policy-101-health-insurance/
- HealthCare.gov glossary: https://www.healthcare.gov/glossary/
- Medicare.gov basics: https://www.medicare.gov/basics
- CMS Medicare Learning Network (free trainings): https://www.cms.gov/outreach-and-education/medicare-learning-network-mln/mlnproducts
- HHS HIPAA for professionals: https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/index.html
- Healthcare Triage (YouTube, accessible health policy)
- "Healthcare.gov explained", "Health Insurance 101" explainers on Investopedia

## Books
- *An American Sickness*, Elisabeth Rosenthal: how the system became expensive (narrative).
- *The Healing of America*, T.R. Reid; *Never Pay the First Bill*, Marshall Allen.
- *Health Insurance Today* (Beik), textbook for AHIP/AHM exams.
- *Understanding Health Insurance*, Michelle Green: textbook for billing/coding.
- *Medical Billing & Coding for Dummies*.
- *Health Informatics: Practical Guide* (Hersh).
- *Fundamentals of Data Engineering*, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, *The Data Warehouse Toolkit* (Kimball).
- *Healthcare Analytics Made Simple* (Vikas Kumar); *Data Analytics in Healthcare*.
- *Medical Claims Adjudication & Analytics* style whitepapers from Milliman/Optum.
