# Customer Service, Appeals, Grievances

## Contact channels
Call centers, portals/apps, chat, mail. Metrics: average speed of answer, first-call resolution, abandonment rate (Stars measure it for MA).

## Appeals vs grievances
- **Appeal**: "I disagree with your coverage/payment decision." Has legal timelines (e.g., member appeals within 60-180 days; decisions in 30 days standard, 72 hrs expedited).
- **Grievance/complaint**: dissatisfaction not about a coverage decision (rude staff, wait times).

## Levels
Internal appeal → (Medicare) Independent Review Entity → ALJ → Medicare Appeals Council → federal court. For commercial plans: internal → external review by independent organization (ACA right).

## Data
`appeal` (case id, claim id, reason, received date, due date, decision, overturn flag), `grievance`, `call_log`. Compliance reports: timeliness by case type, overturn rates, audit samples (CMS program audits sample cases and check letters/timelines).

## Member communications
EOB, ID cards, denial letters, Annual Notice of Change (Medicare), SBC. Must meet readability and language-access rules (Section 1557 nondiscrimination).
