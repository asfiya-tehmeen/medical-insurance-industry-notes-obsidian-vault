# Quality: HEDIS, Stars, CAHPS

## Why quality matters financially
Medicare Advantage **Star Ratings** (1-5) determine bonus payments and marketing rights; 4+ stars is highly valuable. Marketplace has **QRS**; Medicaid has state quality withholds. Accreditation (NCQA) is required by many purchasers.

## HEDIS (Healthcare Effectiveness Data and Information Set)
~90 measures from NCQA. Examples:
- **CBP** controlling blood pressure
- **HBD/GSD** diabetes blood sugar control (HbA1c)
- **BCS** breast cancer screening, **COL** colorectal screening
- **FUH** follow-up within 7 days after mental-health hospitalization
- **PCR** plan all-cause readmissions
- **Childhood/adolescent immunizations**, **well-child visits**

### How a measure works (always the same pattern)
- **Denominator**: eligible members (age, gender, continuous enrollment, diagnosis).
- **Exclusions**: e.g., hospice, mastectomy for BCS.
- **Numerator**: those who got the service (found in claims, labs, EHR/supplemental data).
- **Rate** = numerator ÷ denominator. Gaps in care = in denominator but not numerator → outreach.

### Data sources
Administrative claims, lab results, pharmacy, EHR feeds, state immunization registries, medical record abstraction ("hybrid" method), supplemental data. HEDIS audits verify the process (NCQA-certified auditors). Software must be NCQA-certified; vendors include Cotiviti, Inovalon, Optum.

## Star Ratings pieces
HEDIS, CAHPS member surveys, HOS, Part D drug measures (adherence), operations (call center, appeals timeliness). Weighted, with higher weight for outcomes.

## CAHPS
Member satisfaction surveys (ease of getting care, customer service).

## What data teams do
Build measure logic from specs, run monthly gap lists, trend rates, chase supplemental data feeds, reconcile with audit.
